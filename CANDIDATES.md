# Candidates & Pairwise Tournament — Family Scene (Proud Judge)

Competition: `u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2`
Judge brief criteria, in weight order:
1. A short family scene — real family on the page in a line or two, warm enough to make a proud judge smile.
2. Heartfelt — a small domestic moment that lands with genuine affection, not sentiment stuck on afterwards.
3. Funny in a light, recognisable way — a small turn or a wry line a family would actually laugh at.
4. One or two lines, used well — a whole small scene in a breath, no word wasted.
5. Specific and fresh — one telling detail or an unexpected angle, not a greeting-card line.

## Round 1 (8 matches)

- **M1: C1 vs C9** — Both are warm dad-ritual scenes. C1 wins: the child's shouted comeback is a sharper, more specific turn (criterion 3), and "we both learn to ride that day" lands the warmth (2). C9's closing line is a decent quip but more generic. **Winner: C1.**
- **M2: C2 vs C11** — Both hinge on a child's literal logic. C2 wins: "the rent's due, love" is a funnier, more surprising wry turn (3) and the lipstick detail is more specific (5). C11 is sweet but gentler on both. **Winner: C2.**
- **M3: C3 vs C15** — Two food-tradition scenes. C3 wins: "Twenty years… it's a tradition" carries more genuine affection (2) — a flaw loved into a ritual — where C15's "family portrait" line is a good joke but slightly more constructed (2, 3). **Winner: C3.**
- **M4: C4 vs C7** — C4 wins by a hair: Mum sipping tea while Dad searches is a very specific, recognisable domestic image (5), and her line is wry (3). C7's "he's just early for dinner" is strong, but the scene is a touch more slapstick than warm (2). **Winner: C4.**
- **M5: C5 vs C10** — C5 wins: "He still is" is genuinely heartfelt (2) — a marriage seen through a daughter's eyes in one line. C10 is funny ("grandad just used to hit it", 3) but thinner on warmth; criterion 2 decides before 3. **Winner: C5.**
- **M6: C6 vs C14** — Two grandpa scenes. C6 wins: "Finally. A distinguished gentleman." is the perfect wry line (3) and the image of grandpa admiring a drawn-on moustache is warmer and more specific (2, 5) than the banana gag, which is funny but a one-note joke. **Winner: C6.**
- **M7: C8 vs C13** — C8 wins: "the same petrol and the same attitude" is fresh and specific (5) with a real mother–teen dynamic; C13's "quality-checking… father's duty" is good but a more familiar dad-joke shape. **Winner: C8.**
- **M8: C12 vs C16** — C12 wins: the nightly ritual ("the dragon retires… then does it again tomorrow") has warmth and repetition that feel lived-in (2, 5). C16 is funny but one-note; the extra plate is sweet yet the scene stays a single gag. **Winner: C12.**

## Final

- **F: C3 vs C5** — Both are warm, funny, specific two-line scenes. C3's "it's a tradition" is a beautiful line, but C5 wins on criterion 2 and 5: a daughter trying on her mother's wedding dress, asking if Dad was nervous, and learning he still is — it is the most genuinely affectionate moment in the field, with a wry turn ("he just hides it better now") that a family would actually laugh at, and no word wasted. **CHAMPION: C5.**
